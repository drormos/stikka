# Stikka privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026.

Stikka is a metronome app for Android by Dror Makes, the developer of Stikka on Google Play. It has no ads, no account and no tracking, and your songs stay on your phone.

Stikka was called Metra until version 1.10.0.

**What Stikka collects:** nothing. Stikka doesn't access, collect, use or share personal or sensitive user data. The app has no internet access (it doesn't request Android's internet permission), so it can't send anything anywhere. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no other third-party code that collects or sends data.

**The tuner and the microphone:** Stikka's tuner uses your phone's microphone to hear the note you play. It listens only while the tuner page is open on screen and stops as soon as you leave the page or the app. The sound is analysed on your phone, as it comes in, to find its pitch; it is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

**Who data is shared with:** no one. Stikka shares no data with any party, and sells none.

**What stays on your phone:** your songs, setlists and settings, saved in Stikka's private app storage. Android keeps that storage closed to other apps, and Stikka never sends it anywhere.

**How long it's kept, and deleting it:** your songs, setlists and settings stay until you delete them in the app or uninstall Stikka; uninstalling (or clearing Stikka's storage in your phone's settings) deletes all of them.

**Android backup:** if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Stikka's songs and settings in your Google account backup, so they come back on a new phone. That backup is run by Android and Google under your Google account; Stikka doesn't see or control it. You can turn it off, or delete the backup, in your phone's and Google account's settings.

**Sending feedback:** "Send feedback" in Settings opens your own email app with a message to the developer that starts with Stikka's version, your phone model and your Android version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, from your own email app. Feedback emails are used only to reply and to improve Stikka, aren't shared with anyone, and are deleted on request (write to the address below).

**Permissions:** the only permission Stikka asks you to approve is the microphone, for the tuner, the first time you open the tuner (never at launch); the rest of the app works the same if you refuse it. Stikka doesn't ask for the notification permission. It uses Android's media playback service so the click keeps playing with the screen off; its playback card on the lock screen and in the notification shade is a media control, which Android shows without asking. (Early test builds before version 1.1 asked for the notification permission; current versions don't.)

**Children:** Stikka collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

**Contact:** questions about privacy, or a request about your data, to stikka.app@gmail.com.
